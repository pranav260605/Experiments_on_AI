Finding 1 — The satisfaction gap

Developers who use AI tools report higher job satisfaction. But is the gap big enough to matter?

+1.9pp
AI users are 1.9 percentage points more satisfied than non-AI users
(81.5% vs 79.6%)
Key observation: The gap is driven mostly by "Very satisfied" — AI users are significantly more likely to report peak satisfaction, not just marginal satisfaction.

Finding 2 — Experience vs AI adoption

Do junior developers adopt AI faster? Or do senior developers leverage it more?

Surprise: AI adoption doesn't simply decrease with experience. Mid-career developers (3–10 years) show the highest adoption — they have enough context to use AI effectively, but aren't set in their pre-AI ways.

Finding 3 — India IN vs World AI adoption

How does India compare to the global average and top countries?

India at 60.5% vs world average 53.1% — Indian developers are above the global average in AI tool adoption. With 4,231 respondents, India is one of the largest represented countries in this survey.

Finding 5 — Which AI tools dominate?

ChatGPT leads by a significant margin, but the gap is closing.

Key takeaway

The 1.9pp satisfaction gap is real — but the direction of causality is unclear.

Do AI tools make developers happier? Or do already-happy, engaged developers simply adopt AI faster?

What's certain: 56.9% of AI users don't trust AI output. They use it for speed, not accuracy. That's the real story hiding behind the adoption numbers.
